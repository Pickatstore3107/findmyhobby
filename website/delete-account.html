Delete your account

FindMyHobby app

You can delete your FindMyHobby account and personal data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the FindMyHobby app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you have upcoming bookings, cancel them first (Bookings → your ticket → Cancel slot).

By email

Write to akshaybouroju@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my FindMyHobby account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: your name, email address, phone number and sign-in sessions.
  • Kept without your name: booking and payment records, because Indian tax and accounting law requires businesses to keep them (usually up to 8 years). They are no longer linked to you.
