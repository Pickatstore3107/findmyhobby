How it works

1Pick an activity

Browse today and the next three days.

Up to 3 seats per booking. Pay online or at the venue.

Check-in opens 15 minutes before your slot.

4Play & compete

Join weekend knockouts if you want a challenge.

Competitions

Weekend tournaments

Knockout tournaments for chess, carrom and more, run by the club. Skill-based, with a small entry fee paid at the desk. Results update live in the app.

Prices

Slots are priced per seat per hour and shown before you book — for example, from ₹99 for chess and from ₹199 for yoga or dance. Prices are always shown in the app with all charges included.
