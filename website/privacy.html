Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data FindMyHobby Club (“we”, “us”), operated by Bouroju Akshay, collects through the FindMyHobby app and website, why, and your choices. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in: your name and email address.
  • Phone number, if you add it (required for tournaments so staff can reach you).
  • Bookings: activities, dates, seats, check-ins, tournament entries and results (your name appears in public tournament brackets).
  • Payment records: amount, status and the reference number from our payment partner or your UPI reference. We never see or store your card numbers, UPI PIN or bank passwords — payments are processed by Razorpay.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security and preventing abuse.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, take bookings, confirm payments and check you in.
  • To run tournaments and show results.
  • To process refunds and keep accounting records required by law.
  • To keep the service secure (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay (payment processing) — the details needed to complete a payment or refund.
  • Google — to sign you in.
  • Amazon Web Services — our hosting provider, which stores our database and files.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Where it is stored

Our data is stored on Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Sydney, Australia) region, encrypted in transit. Access is limited to the club's authorised staff.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account; they are then no longer linked to your name.

Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your name and phone number in the app (Me tab).
  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Delete my account. Your name, email and phone are erased immediately.
  • Contact us to access your data, withdraw consent or raise a grievance.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. Children may attend activities when a parent or guardian books for them.
