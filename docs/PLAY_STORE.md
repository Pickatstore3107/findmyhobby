### Data safety form

Data is encrypted in transit: **Yes**. Users can request deletion: **Yes** — in the app (Me → Delete my account) and at **https://findmyhobby.in/delete-account**.

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No | Account management, App functionality | Required |
| Email address | Yes | No | Account management | Required |
| Phone number | Yes | No | App functionality (tournaments, contact on the day) | Optional |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Account management | Required |
| Purchase history (bookings, amounts, payment status) | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |

- **Not collected:** location, contacts, photos, files, messages, app activity analytics, device or advertising IDs, crash logs.
- **Payment details** (card, UPI, bank) are entered on Razorpay's payment page and are never received or stored by FindMyHobby.
- Service providers (Razorpay for payments, Google for sign-in, Supabase and DigitalOcean for hosting) process data on our behalf; under Play's rules this is not "sharing".
